# Official goal: prove faithful procedural retro lifecycles in a fully playable Infipet

## Objective

Extend and verify Infipet’s shared procedural systems until **every creature and valid evolution route in the original international 1997 Digital Monster V1 and vintage English Tamagotchi P1/P2 can be reproduced through a complete, deterministic life using only a legitimate starting drawing, one ordinary seed, and real player care.**

The result must work in the normal playable application, including its care, games, training, battles, health, sleep, aging, persistence, offline simulation and procedural development systems.

**A preset library, successful game counters, passing software tests, or a benchmark framework does not complete this goal. Completion requires actual, independently verifiable reproduction witnesses.**

## 1. Required reference coverage

- Cover all **36 currently identified forms**: 14 Digital Monster V1, 11 P1 and 11 P2.
- Treat the **25 currently cataloged stage paths as a minimum**, not proof that every original route has been captured. Resolve original care-history variants, secret-form requirements and valid alternative endings.
- Pin the exact editions, artwork, stage relationships, care predicates and timing evidence. Resolve the current P2 artwork uncertainty before claiming P2 reproduction.
- Every reference form must appear in a passing whole-life witness. Every verified valid evolution path must have its own passing witness.
- Full-path proofs must begin with the device’s earliest baby drawing. Paths sharing that baby must use the same canonical visible starting artwork; later outcomes may differ through seed and care.
- Record development through the final earned form and subsequent life to a legitimate terminal outcome. Demonstrate death and new creation without automatically inheriting the previous drawing.
- Retain one combined Infipet game, with its declared V1-based lifecycle spine. Verify original-device rules and native clocks through separate reference profiles. Clearly distinguish intentional combined-game adaptations from original behavior.

Later franchise generations and unrelated editions are outside this goal unless explicitly added.

## 2. Procedural integrity: permitted improvements and prohibited shortcuts

**General improvements to the shared engine are authorized**, including anatomy, material redistribution, pigment expression, facial development, movement and developmental timing.

Birth must reproduce the starting artwork exactly. Later development may change pigment, proportions and material arrangement through general rules while preserving traceable source identity and recognizable facial structure. Permanent preservation of every original pixel and color is not required.

The following constraints are mandatory:

- The runtime generator receives only the visible source drawing, generic hints about currently drawn features, ordinary seed, chronological time, real care-derived state and versioned development context.
- Reference artwork and expected outcomes remain confined to research, scoring and comparison tools.
- Do not introduce character-specific templates, sprite replacement, exact-drawing recognizers, character-to-genome lookups or reference branch labels that instruct the renderer what creature to draw.
- Do not conceal future shapes or character-specific programs in seeds, source metadata, invisible pixels, layers, genome payloads, command identifiers, command timing or other indirect channels.
- Do not force care counters, stage commitments, health, training results or battle wins. Every consequence must arise through lawful gameplay.
- One fixed seed must govern the entire witnessed life. Changing seeds, drawings, anatomy or rendering rules between stages invalidates the witness.
- Offline target-aware search is allowed to discover ordinary seeds and playable care routines. Record its sampling method and budget; the deployed simulation must remain unaware of the desired reference.
- New procedural rules must also function on ordinary non-reference drawings. Include counterfactual and previously unseen drawings to detect memorization.
- Care must have genuine causal effects. Demonstrate that changing care can alter eligible development without changing inherited genetics or rewriting previously earned commitments.

Keep the phylogenetic system integrated as a constrained game affinity model. Preserve real ancestry relationships, uncertainty and broad fallbacks. Do not invent biological relationships or character-specific taxonomic exceptions to force a match.

## 3. Definition of a successful reproduction

A passing witness contains **one starting drawing, one seed and one chronological history of actual player commands** that reproduces the entire reference path.

For every stage:

- Earn the correct gameplay branch and reach the comparison checkpoint alive.
- Meet the existing minimum visual gates: **90% foreground ink overlap, 94% filled-silhouette overlap, 94% aspect similarity, 90% density similarity, and matching component/hole topology**.
- Pass separate checks for facial structure, distinguishing features, source continuity and readable anatomy. A high body-overlap score cannot conceal an incorrect face.
- Use checkpoint times, neutral poses and alignment rules fixed before searching. Permit only declared translation and uniform scale; prohibit reflection, rotation, nonuniform warping and per-part fitting.
- Preserve raw frames and inspect relative growth dimensions so normalization cannot conceal incorrect growth.
- Allow a documented common transition interval for continuous maturation. Do not select a different favorable age or animation instant for each candidate.
- Inspect continuous transitions and normal action animations. Matching isolated still frames while producing broken intermediate creatures is insufficient.

Every passing path must receive visual review separate from its automated score. Record the reviewer, images, versions and verdict. Never infer or fabricate human acceptance.

**Every stage must pass under the same recipe.** A collection of unrelated successful adults, exact copied birth drawings, or a favorable average cannot substitute for whole-path reproduction. Do not lower acceptance thresholds to manufacture success.

## 4. Playability, interfaces and reproducibility

- Every reproduced form must function throughout all lawful, age-appropriate care actions, minigames, training, battles, illness/recovery, sleep and mortality scenarios in the normal application.
- Keep one shared editable drawing canvas, untimed creation and an explicit **Begin life** action. Reference reproduction must not require a separate simulation mode with special powers.
- Retain Live/Lab separation, committed live drawings and seeds, preserved future care keyframes, and stable stage commitments.
- Keep versioned witness and portable-save interfaces containing the starting source, mode, seed, clock, real command history and all relevant engine/catalog versions. Store reference targets and evaluation results separately.
- Identical versioned inputs must reproduce gameplay outcomes and rendered frames across fresh processes, import/export, reloads, cache states and seek orders.
- Inherited genetics must remain independent of care. Rendering and seeking must not consume mutable randomness.
- Stable action identities must prevent duplicate rewards and rerolled outcomes across persistence and replay.
- Uninterrupted and offline simulation must agree except for the explicitly declared, persisted death-grace behavior. Reopening must not replenish that grace.
- Preserve the separation between chronological care time and anatomical maturation, including the existing six-hour and seven-day care responses.
- Follow prototype policy: introduce a new revision when required, reset only `infipet.*`, and add no migrations or backups unless requested.

## 5. Evidence required before declaring completion

Deliver:

- A complete, edition-qualified coverage ledger listing every form, valid route, evidence source and remaining uncertainty.
- At least one passing whole-life witness for every required route, with all 36 forms covered.
- Portable recipes that replay successfully through both automated verification and the ordinary app.
- Per-stage scores, raw frames, transition captures, visual-review records, gameplay histories, version pins and integrity hashes.
- Negative and counterfactual tests demonstrating that reference data cannot influence runtime generation and that drawing, seed and care each have legitimate effects.
- Passing TypeScript checks, relevant existing regressions, the full test suite, web export and iOS JavaScript export.
- Phone-width visual verification. Report signed iOS, physical-device and widget testing separately; do not imply those passed without performing them.

**Do not mark this goal complete while any required creature or route lacks a passing witness, reference uncertainty prevents certification, or an integration failure remains.** Report partial progress and blockers explicitly. Search exhaustion is evidence about the attempted search, not proof of success or impossibility.
