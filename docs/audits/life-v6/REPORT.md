## What changed

V6 separates immutable authored material, changing anatomical regions, and functional movement. The seed compiles inherited body-plan alternatives and overlapping developmental operations. Needs select alternatives only when each operation starts, using history available then. Future care events cannot change earlier development.

The renderer transports source masks and uses signed-distance operations for new tissue, cavities, mass division/fusion, and absorption. It does not crossfade finished monster sprites. Faces, pigments, semantic markings, and floating ownership retain source identities. Bright eyes carry their authored negative-space neighborhood. Physical ear/horn/tail/hair roots follow actual host material, including intermediate field blends; intentional floating parts are not snapped to hosts. Distant floating ink is included in the common camera fit before final viewport clipping.

Days **0/30/75/120** are infant/teen/adult/elder milestones, with continuous restructuring between them. The timeline still reaches240; care-driven remodeling continues without a death mechanic. Eggs crack and reveal a seeded hatchling before this developmental clock begins. Source edits retain the seed and care recipe and return to the beginning.

Inherited routes account for the initial support anatomy and avoid successive potential configurations with the same broad organization. The six editable sources are Bean, Sprout, Cub, Ribbon, Beetle, and Wisp. They are original monochrome pocket monsters, not adult templates. Source colors remain supported.

The functional graph supplies eight held LCD poses, role-based four-beat quadruped steps, fixed-length two-link IK, stance contacts, intrinsic spine waves, and other movement families. Abilities depend on mature structures; fin labels do not grant flight. Activities remain animation demonstrations and do not secretly change care values.

All67 needs,48 feelings,56 separate faux-feeling interpretations, and the pinned offline associations remain in the model. Six need profiles currently affect appearance. Other needs remain active emotional clues and are explicitly unmodeled structurally. The single drawing canvas and compact Draw/Live/More interface remain; internal graph identifiers stay in diagnostics and audits.

### Uncertainty and care

Two thousand deterministic whole-seed bootstrap replicates give conditional95% intervals of **68.5–83.5%** for adult shape separation, **82.0–91.9%** for distinct lifecycle classification, and **93.9–98.6%** for primary clear-evolution scores. [The analysis script](bootstrap-analysis.mjs) resamples shared seed blocks across sources, ages, and histories, uses weighted distinct-original-seed pairs, and equally weights the source summaries. It does not pretend correlated frames or pairs are independent players. These intervals do not include reviewer error or prove a global uniqueness rate.

In the four-history adult descriptor panel, genetic variation supplies approximately80.9% of the within-source sum-of-squares share averaged across six sources; history supplies2.2% and seed–history interaction16.8%. Raw sums and denominators are retained in metrics.json. This includes severe Safety shortage and is a descriptive panel, not a population or moderate-care causal estimate.

Safety can select visibly different inherited later forms. Its response depends on genotype and accumulated history; it does not always create the same shell. However, many within-family changes remain below the5% normalized shape threshold. The fully tended Rest/Fitness comparison is especially subtle structurally. Emotional and movement responses are separate measurements; they must not be advertised as equivalent to strong morphological responses.

TypeScript, web export, iOS Hermes export, and plain iOS JavaScript export pass. Logs are saved as `verification-*.txt`. Browser testing at390×844 verifies the compact controls, inline Needs Lab, preserved timeline during rewind/reroll, source-edit rewind, new-pet clearing, and reload persistence. [UI details](UI-VERIFICATION.md) distinguish actual browser checks from automated tests.

The final full capture measures Node frame generation at approximately 3.47 ms median and 6.41 ms p95, with 99.9% (1,535/1,536) under 16 ms and 100% (1,536/1,536) under 50 ms across the scheduled ages. These exclude React/native rendering. The [final separate performance probe](profiling/runtime-final.json) measured the dense 15,360-cell fixture at 23.04 ms structural p95 and 5.90 ms posed p95; blueprint occupancy stayed at eight over 13/13 bounded probes. Internal frame/checkpoint limits are declared separately from observed cache telemetry.

Only the proof/three-body-plan runs contain sampled intermediate growth and full motion loops. Full-population continuity scores describe endpoint lineage plausibility, not inspection of every tween or gait. Agent reviews are not human reviews; no human-review credit is claimed. Where later captures are pixel-identical to already inspected images, transfer receipts retain the original inspection and exact equivalence evidence instead of inventing a new review.

Physical iPhone performance, native interaction, signed installation, and WidgetKit scheduling remain separate device checks. Widgets still consume care-conditioned rendered frames at the controlled age; closing the app does not advance development or needs.

## Prioritized next improvements

1. **More variety within inherited families.** Increase mass-layout, head/trunk relationships, asymmetry, spine configurations, and covering structure combinations. The recurring pillar, ring, and heavy quadruped families are the main near-twin source. Adding arbitrary appendages is not the remedy.
2. **Eliminate weak stage pairs.** Add source-conditioned morphological distance/readability constraints to the inherited grammar, especially low horizontal/pear forms. Use the40 preserved failures as regression cases; keep raw seeds fixed.
3. **Stronger facial readability through radical changes.** Preserve compact authored clusters without crowding, oversized outlines, or tiny peripheral faces. Keep negative-space and floating-intent contracts.
4. **More distinctive modeled care responses.** Strengthen plausible region-specific fullness, reinforcement, guarded curvature, and recovery without allowing care to swamp genetic variety. Keep the other61 needs emotionally active until there is a clear structural model.
5. **Motion handoffs and source inference.** Improve unusual views and unfamiliar authored anatomy, preserve readable support during reorganization, and inspect actual loops. Numerical mode selection is not gait quality.
6. **Physical iPhone/widget validation.** Measure native rendering and pathological drawings before promising a device frame-rate budget.
