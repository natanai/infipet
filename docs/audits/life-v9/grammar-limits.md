# Procedural grammar: capabilities and open gaps

The lifecycle benchmarks test whether a starting drawing, ordinary seed and recorded care can generate a reference path. The reference targets are evaluation data. The renderer does not receive character identifiers, target masks, reference ancestry or future sprites. No near-exact roster coverage is claimed by adding the following operators.

## Added grammar

`morphogenesis-v1` is recorded in inherited genome metadata and in development/program cache identity. Existing inherited random channels retain their values; the new channels have their own versioned namespace.

- A seed-derived 20% population can retain a simple infant body envelope and low curvature. This expression applies to bodies without authored heads or load-bearing supports. Detailed authored structures continue their established regional development.
- A declared sheltered lifecycle still forms a complete enclosing shell at its peak. Conservative body retention affects the body envelope, not the shell's coverage requirement.
- Source-rooted buds can develop supports with feet, manipulators with hands, tails, paired terminal outgrowths and ears. Their stages, proportions and availability are inherited; care can modulate expression. Actual occupied source material supplies roots, pigment and ancestry. Existing limbless potential continues to suppress new legs.
- A seed-derived 40% population allows bounded eye/mouth scale, spacing and lift for faces embedded directly in a body. The operator transports the authored ink. Explicit heads retain their exact feature-to-head mapping; deliberately floating faces retain their carriers.
- Every addition fades in after birth. Original birth artwork, source identities, protected facial colors and arbitrary-seek reproducibility remain covered by tests. Generated structures share the existing finite region budget.

## Filled bodies, outlines and pigment contrast

A filled infant becoming an outlined later form is still a concrete missing capability. The existing material rasterizer transports and expands authored pigment. It does not yet have a general rule for consuming or reallocating a filled region's interior material into a hollow rim, or for changing surface pigment while preserving independently readable facial contrast.

Deleting interior pixels as an audit-only mask would bypass the developmental model. Filling that interior with a secondary pigment would also hide same-colored eyes unless the model explicitly defined pigment expression and feature contrast. A target-specific silhouette, palette swap or stored later sprite would not demonstrate that the seed grammar can produce the transition.

Future work should introduce a generic, causal surface-differentiation operation: explicit material resorption/redistribution or pigment expression, with bounded stage timing, source lineage, feature-contrast semantics and tests. The reference litmus should measure this gap before choosing that mechanism. No special exception for an individual creature belongs in the renderer.

## Other limits

Terminal outgrowths currently use a small geometric vocabulary. Arbitrary armor plates, teeth, internal LCD markings and clade-specific anatomy are not established capabilities. Broad ancestral affinity biases are not anatomical reconstructions. The shared anatomical clock has three main developmental intervals; original devices have different stage schedules. Reference timing alignment and genuine game-rule execution must remain separate evidence.

An average silhouette score can conceal a failed intermediate stage. Whole-path results must retain stage-level scores, worst-stage quality, topology/feature checks, readable contact sheets and a reproducible input recipe. Missing targets or unknown care requirements are unscored; an unsuccessful seed search is a failure to demonstrate coverage, not evidence that a hidden successful seed exists.

## Validation

Five new grammar tests pass, covering inherited purity/versioning, conservative and expressive populations, complete sheltered enclosure, new anatomy from body/face-only sources, protected facial ink and seek replay. The final enclosure/focused suite passed 91 tests and a separate grooming/lineage/cache/care suite passed 70 tests. The final TypeScript check passed. These are software regression checks, not visual or human acceptance of near-exact reference paths.
