Run: **v7-regional-proof-003**. Reviewer: agent /root/starters_v6, source-author aware and unblinded. Human review remains pending.

All **1,040/1,040 frozen care images** were inspected through 24 [matched comparison sheets](comparison-manifest.json), yielding 192 seed-level 14-day comparisons. Runtime was not evaluated or edited. The raw pixel-difference pass rate is not a visual legibility score.

All **24/24 shortage need/onset groups** meet the at least 3/4 clear-seed requirement by 14 days. Fast guarding, bracing, anterior withdrawal and tension are now readable alongside slower tissue/fullness changes. Early Rest/Fitness remain the weakest cohorts: seed 3 stays subtle at both infant onsets. Some elder seed 2 responses are also restrained. Appearance still does not uniquely identify a specific unmet need.

## Scoring

0=no reliably visible difference;1=subtle contour/fullness/posture;2=clear relevant response;3=strong coherent remodeling. Headline counts require **score>=2**. Every seed comparison used all five sampled offsets and its matched control; early frames support sequence review and have no standalone score.
