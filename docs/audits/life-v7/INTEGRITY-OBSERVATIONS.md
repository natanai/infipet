## Frozen proof004 diagnostic observations

The [machine-readable extract](INTEGRITY-OBSERVATIONS.json) records the exact capture hash and source-code hashes. Its scope is one authored Cub, four published raw seeds, 16 milestone frames, 60 intermediate frames, and 128 sampled moving poses: 204 frames total. These are observations from the selected proof, not a household-pet success percentage.

- 204/204 frames pass the recorded coordinate/pixel validity checks; 4/4 cases report seek agreement and future-event causality.

- No sampled view reports missing source identities or host mismatches. These lineage checks are separate from visible contribution and visual coherence.
- Across 128 moving poses, maximum bone-length error is 0.000769 world units and maximum contact error is 0.000474. Final viewport diagnostics report zero outside pixels.
- The nominal ground-reference diagnostic reaches 0.7002 world units on six flutter poses: seed 4 at days 30/120, phases 0/1/7. All listed feet are unplanted. This comes from flight bob relative to the reference floor; it must not be summarized as universally zero ground penetration or mistaken for a planted walking-foot failure.
- The sampled rendered movement modes are walk, hop and flutter. A finite eight-phase contact sheet does not demonstrate continuity at every animation time or readability of every blink.

The observed head defect in source proof003 and the zero-time animation cache collision were real failures despite earlier metadata/part-mask checks. Their focused regressions and preserved before/after evidence strengthen the current claim; they also show why numerical invariants and agent gates cannot replace actual image review or the user's visual judgement. Web/iOS JavaScript exports remain separate from signed iOS installation, physical iPhone rendering, and WidgetKit behavior.
