# Life v7 implementation: authored material, inherited genes, and causal care

Algorithm/schema **7**, revision **life-v7**, app **0.7.0**, needs rules **2**, applied adaptation profiles **2** (`adaptation-rules.ts`). This describes working mechanisms, not a completed visual result. Human rejection of the earlier mostly stretched lifecycle overrides its agent acceptance. The current regional-anatomy rework has disposable previews and separately frozen candidates, but has not passed a new human visual gate. Proof002 remains the failed-care baseline. Visual findings belong to the separately versioned [audit index](INDEX.md); passing code tests is not a visual gate.

## The implemented biological metaphor

The model consists of persistent source-material regions, coordinated regional growth cages, source-contour tissue growth, explicit topology operations, a relative remodeling budget, and two-dimensional kinematic contacts. Its need-to-anatomy mappings are fictional gameplay profiles. The canonical NVC catalog supplies vocabulary and feeling–need associations; it does not establish that a psychological need produces a particular anatomical structure.

Here, inherited potential means seeded coefficients and permitted operations. Selection means conditional expression of those options under recorded care. The implementation does not simulate breeding populations, molecular genetics, conserved physical mass/energy, or measured biological fitness. The relative tissue budget introduces functional tradeoffs without claiming those physical properties.

## Separate source, genome, program, and resolved state

| Representation        | What it contains                                                                                                                          | What can change it                                  |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Inherited genome      | Seed/mode fingerprint, stage coefficients, local gene fields, support/membrane/division potential, motion preferences                     | Seed, beginning mode, algorithm revision            |
| Source atlas          | Actual typed cells, dimensions, width profiles, centerlines, density, components/cavities, roots, surface ports, protected features       | Authored geometry and semantic hints                |
| Developmental program | The atlas bound to inherited operations, source-material partitions, coordinated mass layouts, scheduled windows, ports and finite limits | Source edits or inherited genome                    |
| Resolved development  | Causal decisions, independently transported material cages, tissue bands, current care mediators, allocations and readiness               | Requested age and preceding care history            |
| Functional pose       | Role-based contacts, joints, spine waves and effectors                                                                                    | Current anatomy, action and explicit animation time |

The genome is compiled from **seed + mode + life-v7**, not the source pixels or need history. Changing a drawing, ID, name, pigment or care event therefore does not produce a new genome. Source and program fingerprints are distinct diagnostics. The atlas geometry digest is a typed-cell geometry summary, not a complete saved-document content hash; audit manifests separately hash the actual source and code.

Local inherited fields vary smoothly over material position. Editing a tail or body proportion changes how that source binds to the fixed fields; it can change developmental expression without avalanching every inherited coefficient. Source ordering and renamed material IDs do not supply growth randomness. A reroll intentionally changes the genome while retaining drawing, chronology and care history.

There are no finished adult family sprites. The main authored body or egg is partitioned into up to three persistent material domains along its original centerline. Each owns a disjoint subset of the actual source cells, including their pigments, paint depths and negative space. Clipped `Tissue` objects are immutable and reused between ages; their original source identity is retained, and the central domain keeps the original body region ID. Tiny sources use fewer domains. Other authored parts keep their own source material.

Inherited stage agendas change the relative centers, radii, orientations and mass balance of those domains across the full infant→teen→adult→elder intervals. Source anatomy constrains the available mass and support arrangements. A domain maps through its own cage; the torso does not merely bend along one shared axis. Source-relative end-cap taper changes its contour without replacing the mask with an ellipse. The earlier large centerline-curvature transform is disabled as the primary silhouette mechanism; its transport helper remains available for local interpolation and tests. Fine care, remodeling and grooming fields still operate on material after the regional arrangement.

The renderer forward-maps authored and generated occupied cell areas through the cage and pose, rather than rotating sparse rounded point samples. Membrane triangle–cell intersection uses a separating-axis test. Working raster coordinates are bounded to±256; material outside the final64×64 world is retained until the shared, pose-independent camera fit. The final viewport clip and its diagnostics happen afterward. This prevents an early world clip from silently shortening wings before the camera can see them.

It also adds a bounded outer distance-field band in original source coordinates, inheriting the nearest source pigment and depth. The original full mask protects enclosed cavities even when a domain cut opens a cavity locally; component separation protects authored gaps. Every domain, including its added band, uses its fixed cage so new band samples cannot jump into a neighboring domain. Authored source pixels paint over the new tissue band. Generated structures choose pigment from the nearest occupied, mapped host source cell at their growth root, with geometry-only ties; generated descendants without their own source inherit that root pigment. Palette and array order do not choose the anatomy.

Broad waists connect transported domains only where their original occupied material was adjacent. Separate contact patches around a cavity produce separate seams; nearby but disconnected drawing fragments do not acquire a waist merely because their current positions are close. Connectors use actual transported source-skin endpoints. Secondary authored cores can still undergo disjoint source-clip division or an explicitly selected fusion bridge carrying both source identities. Absorption excludes protected descendants. Allowed new supports or membranes originate at source-derived roots and gain readiness gradually; an upright arrangement can instead reassign existing fore-support material to reaching roles.

Eyes, mouths, markings, hollow-head coordinates and intentional floating ownership are protected. A surface marking follows the material domain beneath each of its cells. Articulated children inherit a local rigid frame at the corresponding material root, rather than the host's distant scale or shear. Floating parts keep their logical carrier and authored gap. Heads use bounded uniform growth and damped orientation instead of torso pinching. Body-hosted facial islands carry their glyphs and contrasting skin together through a shared guarded frame. Physical heads can move both inward and outward toward occupied host skin around their authored contact; an unintended long tether is not preserved merely because it is outside the body. Explicit authored neck tissue and floating gaps remain separate cases. Generated neck width follows head size. A neck terminal blocked by facial glyphs or isolated on diagonal-only skin is rerouted to the edge-connected skin on its authored contact side. This preserves the black glyphs instead of painting over them to hide a gap. Its regression checks the final composited skin, since an underlying part-mask union can incorrectly count skin covered by a black nose or mouth. Protected facial windows and skin are resolved with the posed material. These are invariants and regression targets, not proof that every possible drawing remains visually coherent. One-pixel eyes have deliberate blink clusters. Authored infant pixels and paint depth are exact at idle birth; empty sources remain empty.

The seed-only life program also permits a sheltered interval. Its two coating regions derive from the current body outline; physical appendages fold toward their roots and can contract to20% length before releasing on emergence. Their source cells remain compiled throughout. The face remains outside the occluding cover through a protected opening. Internal mass and available support roles continue reorganizing under the coating; the coating itself is not evidence of a new adult form. Limbless sources do not require invented limbs to complete that interval.

The drawing stays 32×32 and the rendered world 64×64. Current program bounds are 32 generated regions, 64 functional bones, and eight simultaneous operations. Source fragments can share control while retaining their cells and gaps. All rigs, ports and lineage identifiers stay outside the ordinary player interface.

Egg mode preserves the shell while a seeded hatchling emerges during 4–10 days of incubation. That generated infant becomes the developmental source atlas. The shell need not resemble it; hatchling colors sample the egg palette. Infant mode instead starts directly from the authored drawing.

## Six independent needs and a finite allocation budget

Every one of the 67 needs retains fulfillment, a six-hour fulfillment average, cumulative deficit-days, and seven-day deficit memory. The six structurally modeled needs additionally expose independent read-only drivers. With `D(f)=clamp((.75-f)/.75,0,1)`, their chronic load is `.55 × D(shortTerm) + .45 × memory`. Positive opportunity is `clamp((shortTerm-.75)/.25,0,1)`.

The applied version2 profiles separate fast expression from slow tissue adaptation. Guarding, bracing, head/effector exposure, tension, support recruitment and movement pace use the continuous six-hour shortage/opportunity directly. A raw dial change cannot instantaneously remold the pet. Actual tissue fullness, reinforcement and restoration use the additional days-long morphology mediators and retained memory. `activeNeedSignals` records the raw fast channel; `needSignals` records the raw slow channel. Raw fulfillment and acute deficit remain observable without inventing adaptation before birth.

| Need             | Main regional rule                                                                   | Declared functional tradeoff                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Safety           | Fast compatible guarding/compaction; slow reinforcement of existing exposed material | Protective allocation can reduce reach or movement range          |
| Security         | Fast material-root bracing; slow support thickness and load placement                | Wider support uses allocation and can restrict stride/flexibility |
| Trust            | Fast head overlap/exposure and rooted effector reach; slow retained openness         | Openness exposes surface; withdrawal reduces reach                |
| Rest             | Fast fatigue/resting posture and pace; slow cross-section fullness and restoration   | Repair competes with motion and other material demands            |
| Relaxation       | Fast held bend/tension; slower retained curvature                                    | Bracing trades flexibility for held posture                       |
| Physical fitness | Fast support recruitment; slow chest/pelvis and existing-effector reinforcement      | Larger functional structures require material and support         |

The source and genotype determine which responses are anatomically available. Inherited protection preference selects a compatible Safety expression rather than a universal shell. Trust moves the whole head/face carrier and existing effectors within physical contact and exposure bounds. Rest/Fitness shortage weights are0.45 for the declared slow fullness/load-bearing channels, with fast fatigue/recruitment weights0.28; these are coefficients before allocation and geometric bounds, not promised on-screen size changes. Their positive gain coefficients remain0.30/0.32. These profiles change existing tissue, without adding limbs to advertise a need.

Only Trust, Rest and Fitness have extra positive structural expression above neutral75%. Safety, Security and Relaxation can release acquired guarding/tension during recovery, but100% does not force a new positive shape from an otherwise neutral history. Other61 needs remain emotional inputs with no structural mapping. These are declared fictional profiles, not NVC-derived anatomy claims.

The already inherited sensitivity trait now scales each applied need contribution once using `clamp(1+(sensitivity-1)*.5,.85,1.15)`. It affects response strength, including head/effector, tissue/allocation and movement contributions; it changes neither the raw signals nor the six-hour, seven-day or morphology clocks. It does not reseed genetics or change which conditional mutation/operation branch commits. Diagnostics expose the inherited/effective multiplier and separate applied magnitudes. Zero signals retain exact neutral geometry and motion settings.

The slower morphology mediator half-lives are respectively **4, 9, 7, 2, 3, and 8 days**. They become slower through the elder transition. Each hourly target advances the following interval, so a future dial change cannot influence preceding fractions of an hour. Ongoing mediators affect material between decision windows and after maturation; they do not merely select a branch once.

Physical regions request relative remodeling resources, with need-dependent priorities and a bounded available budget. Allocation constrains regional expression; minimum capacity prevents a fully unmet condition from stalling the entire life cycle. Diagnostics expose requested/allocated resources; the separate regional-profile catalog declares functional costs. The units are relative game units, not calories, grams or a physical simulation.

Hair length and curvature inherit variation. Hair density and pigment hue/saturation/brightness channels are reserved separately, with no dedicated need mapping yet. Authored pigments remain the source; registering a channel does not claim that it has an active gameplay rule.

## Open-ended time and deterministic long seeks

Days **0/30/75/120** remain infant/teen/adult/elder milestones. `maxAge` and `initialPreviewEnd` describe the initial 240-day preview (plus egg incubation); `openEnded` is true. They are not evaluation caps. The Time drawer can extend the window by **+240d**, and finite chronological ages beyond it remain valid.

After day120, bounded non-affine remodeling continues on `log1p(remodelingAge/90)`. Its rate slows toward zero. Senescence approaches an asymptote and lowers movement pace/amplitude toward nonzero floors. There is no death flag or terminal day. No further organ-generation milestones are scheduled after the existing three transitions, while slow material change and care remain active.

Care keyframes are nonnegative safe-integer hours after birth/hatching. Constant-condition need segments integrate analytically between events. Morphology preserves the former left-hour-sampled filters through day240 with at most a fixed 5,760-hour prefix. Later rates are constant, permitting exact geometric convolution between events, split where fast fulfillment crosses the neutral threshold. Seeking a million days ahead therefore costs event processing rather than millions of hourly steps. Need and morphology checkpoint maps are each bounded to 32 entries; canonical integration boundaries keep results independent of query order.

The numeric safety boundary is `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` care hours, not a designed lifespan. Nonfinite input is not a meaningful chronological age. Sharing records chronology and the care recipe rather than materializing every intervening hour.

Animation time is explicit and nonnegative. The pose controller and whole-frame cache share the same held-phase calculation. The cache also distinguishes exact time zero from positive time, because birth rendering and blink activation have different zero-time behavior. A discovered cache collision had returned unblinked eyes for a warmed epsilon-time query, then changed the same query after eviction; the [failure and corrected replay reports](cache-replay-v1/) retain that evidence. Cold, warmed and post-eviction epsilon queries now agree across the sampled ages, including idle birth. Bounded caches are optimization state, not additional simulation inputs.

Environment-driven care, activity-driven care and rejuvenation/de-aging are future work. The current clocks provide maturation and remodeling state without a de-aging control. Any future biological-age intervention must retain chronological age and all recorded care events.

## Directional grooming prototype

The small Live grooming tool records chronological `GroomStroke` commands: source ID, safe-integer hour, original-material anchor, canonical direction, strength, and optional gesture ID. It retains the source drawing and need fulfillment. Undo/redo changes this separate input history. New pets clear it; source edits, rerolls, imports and reloads retain it. Removed source IDs may leave dormant commands rather than silently redirecting them.

The backend accepts at most one unit of grooming input per chronological day, allocated in causal order. A gesture has zero immediate growth effect, a 2.5-day response rise and a 28-day decay. Length bias is bounded to25%, bend to20°, and allocation-priority bias to8%, each limited by inherited opportunities. These are modifier bounds, not promised changes in on-screen extent. Surface correspondence and explicit ancestry aliases carry commands through allowed material changes; protected facial features are excluded from direct grooming.

The [grooming evidence status](../../grooming-v1/README.md) is provisional. The old64-frame candidate whose source hashes changed remains unsuitable as frozen acceptance. Separately named current bounded proofs retain their own hashes and visual receipts. After the attachment and animation-cache corrections, exact comparison retained all11 lifecycle images, their11 core and11 uncovered diagnostics, and all64 grooming images and64 target-contributor masks. Additive [lifecycle](lifecycle/v7-regional-lifecycle-002/final-runtime-equivalence.json) and [grooming](../../grooming-v1/v7-regional-groom-002/final-runtime-equivalence.json) transfer receipts preserve those observations without rewriting frozen captures. Grooming remains a subtle directional bias at native64, not a dramatic manual morph. Numerical gene/needs equality and changed pixels do not resolve the broader human rejection of the lifecycle's dramatic-evolution result. Expanded collection remains gated on reviewed current evidence.

## Movement, evidence, and current limits

Developed anatomy supplies functional roles and readiness. Authored fore/hind/side roles are compiled from source geometry rather than guessed from each later pose. Sequential role operations can return transformed arms/hands to supports during a later stage. Eligible spring-support plans drive actual hopping with existing ready supports; a label alone grants no controller. Quadrupeds use four footfall phases and fixed-length two-link IK; other families use their own support, spine or wing mechanics. Eight held LCD phases are sampled from explicit animation time. The camera transform includes the full creature and attachments and stays independent of individual pose extents.

The posed-material bank is bounded by240,000 stored pixels and512 entries and resets on developmental-age changes. This is separate from structural/frame caches. Cache occupancy is not a device frame-rate claim; unusually dense valid drawings still cost more to rasterize.

The regional compiler is a bounded two-dimensional construction, not a learned anatomy model or an unlimited biological search. Its inherited layout vocabulary and permitted generated structures are finite. Source-contour growth preserves material correspondence, but folded or overlapping regions can still visually obscure negative space or motifs. Independent masks, generated seams and limb controllers can produce awkward silhouettes, crowded faces or unclear support roles; those require actual rendered review. Formal lineage, non-affine point motion and connected pixels do not establish convincing metamorphosis, readable faces or appropriate gait. Small-source/native64 care cues can remain subtle, even when enlarged comparisons or movement pace differ. Current small-slice and supplemental receipts remain distinct from a human gate and from any later expanded cohort.

Use [current verification](../../VERIFICATION.md) for completed checks and pending exports/reviews. The [provisional pre-rework Node profile](profiling/v7-grooming-final.json) separates structural evaluation, first generation of eight poses, cached calls, and observed blueprint occupancy. Dense valid drawings remain substantially slower than the starter examples; cached pose timings are not fresh-render or device frame-rate claims.

The source-audit rubric requires dramatic evolution and descent from the drawing independently. It retains unreviewed cases, fixed raw seeds, source counterfactuals, care histories, intermediate frames and motion evidence. Source IDs, changed pixels and biological terminology alone are insufficient evidence of a convincing pet. Physical iPhone interaction, native rendering performance, signed installation and WidgetKit scheduling remain separate checks.
