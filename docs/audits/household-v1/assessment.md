## Saved checkpoint — stopped at the user's request

The current growth system falls well short of the desired visual variety. The next target is **transformations as dramatic as Pokémon, Digimon, and other monster-evolution games, produced procedurally pixel by pixel over time**. Growth should substantially reorganize silhouettes, proportions, and anatomy, rather than mainly enlarging or tilting the original drawing. This direction supersedes tuning the present proportion-based growth system.

Saved work includes ten editable presets, all **322,560 primary snapshots**, the frozen source/seed/care matrix, **4,600 primary visually reviewed frames**, **920 independently second-reviewed frames**, all supplemental checks, and **80 reviewed motion sheets**. The growth engine itself was not changed. The report and raw evidence are retained as a baseline of the current system's limitations.

**Statistics are a draft checkpoint.** The last complete saved summary predates final statistical corrections. Pair-bootstrap normalization and late-stress labeling fixes are written in the tooling, but their final full recomputation was interrupted on request. Principal-axis posture responses also require an anisotropy gate before they can support care-response conclusions. Some per-preset analysis caches contain later calculations than the complete summary. Do not use the saved confidence intervals or composite care-response percentages as final validated results. The rendered images and recorded visual reviews remain valid evidence of insufficient transformation.

The completed numerical comparisons already show nearby silhouette families across every preset, consistent with visual review. Zero exact image-sequence collisions does not establish a different-looking experience for each player. Semantic ratio changes and small raster topology changes also do not establish interesting evolution.

No additional audit, engine tuning, or transformation implementation is continuing after this checkpoint. Physical iPhone and signed-widget verification were not performed.
