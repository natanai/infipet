# Classic game rules and evidence

This prototype supplies one combined Infipet game. It implements feeding, four-heart meters, weight, hygiene, illness and repeated medicine, sleep and lights, discipline and refusals, five-round games, training, CPU battles, stage predicates, death, and deterministic offline catch-up. It is **not yet a verified full hardware replica**. The biological renderer continues to draw the player's creature; reference monster names identify internal rule slots, not replacement sprites.

The references are international English 1997 Digital Monster Version 1 and vintage Tamagotchi P1/P2. A later product sold as “Digimon Original” can be the substantially different Version 20th. A modern Tamagotchi rerelease manual is not sufficient evidence for a vintage P2 number algorithm.

## Explicit Infipet defaults and adaptations

- An egg hatches after **five minutes**. Subsequent stage boundaries are **1, 49, 121 and 193 chronological hours after hatching**. Only the first one-hour interval is corroborated here. Sleeping time contributes to this combined game's stage schedule.
- Hunger loses a heart each awake hour; happiness and strength each lose one per 90 minutes. Waste is produced every three awake scheduled hours. Sleeping skips depletion events. Exact device-specific rates, minimum weights, scheduled baby sickness, sleep schedules and senescence acceleration are not yet calibrated.
- Sleep runs 21:00–07:00 at the UTC offset captured when life starts. Timezone changes and daylight-saving shifts do not rewrite that clock. Turning off lights is required for the bedtime call. This is a fixed combined-game schedule, not all species' original schedules.
- Gameplay rules **2** allow turning lights off to return a disturbed pet to its scheduled sleep immediately. Rules 1 incorrectly forced it awake until morning, preventing the recorded nine-disturbance branch from being earned within the infant interval. A regression earns nine disturbances through actual training-entry, cancellation and lights commands. The sleep-resume timing is an explicit Infipet adaptation awaiting original-device calibration.
- Four accumulated waste piles followed by three dirty hours trigger sickness. Prolonged hunger is lethal after eight hours; untreated sickness after twelve; an untreated injury after six. These are declared game-design values. The V1 source says up to four waste piles alone do not affect care mistakes; sanitation here comes from the combined Tamagotchi-style game.
- A pet has a **30-day chronological maximum lifespan**. This prototype limit is not an original-device lifespan claim. It bounds hourly derived care to at most 721 samples / 4,326 need keyframes and makes even an extremely long absence cheap to replay.
- Every absence processes neglect and stage checks. A lethal offline event creates a persisted **30 cumulative foreground-minute** grace budget. Reopening cannot replenish an unresolved episode. Treating all lethal conditions can clear the episode; old age instead provides time to say goodbye. An actively neglected pet receives no new return grace.
- Five-round high/low training and named CPU opponents provide playable local substitutes for the original training presentation and physical connector battles. CPU win and injury formulas are deterministic Infipet rules, not emulated original firmware.
- Games award one heart for at least three correct answers. Canceled training still counts as a training entry; other canceled games award nothing. An unresolved minigame is abandoned during an absence without reward. Completed battle results remain committed when the app closes; presentation and reopening cannot reroll them.
- False attention calls and low-discipline refusals exist. Exact vintage refusal probabilities and discipline scheduling remain provisional. Snacks and protein add weight; an exact original snack-mortality mechanism has not been reproduced.

## Determinism, persistence and biological coupling

The pure engine accepts explicit timestamps and command IDs. Due timers execute before a command at the same timestamp. It never reads `Date.now`, consumes mutable randomness, or depends on rendering. Fixed-point energy makes irregular replay equal to a single catch-up call. Backward wall-clock movement never rewinds a life. The last 256 command IDs prevent duplicate command delivery; the latest 512 readable events are retained while cumulative and stage counters remain authoritative.

The game saves a serializable state together with its clock anchor, accepted command receipts, stage commitments and remaining grace budget. The app serializes saves, checkpoints foreground time every five seconds and saves during background transitions. An abrupt process kill can lose at most one ordinary checkpoint interval; this is a local disposable prototype, not an anti-cheat system.

Biological age, gameplay chronology and animation time are separate. `deriveDevelopmentContext` provides the stage schedule and committed care/training/battle metrics. `deriveCareTimeline` samples six modeled needs only on exact chronological hour boundaries after hatching; fractional-hour actions cannot rewrite a past keyframe. Manual Lab keyframes stay independent. The adapter's fulfillment formulas are fictional physiology, not a biological claim or the devices' original meters.

## Verification and remaining parity work

Gameplay tests cover precise hatch and stage boundaries, batch/irregular replay equality, overlapping calls, live and offline mortality, preserved grace across reopen, recovery, medicine doses, canceled training, all minigames, CPU outcome idempotence, branch boundaries, the advanced gate, a cared-for complete lifespan, million-day absence and malformed imports. These are software checks, not comparison traces from original hardware.

Before claiming full reference parity, obtain pinned scans of the original English manuals and controlled vintage-device traces, then resolve the provisional timings, sleeping/evolution clock behavior, exact decay tables, illness/death criteria, P2 ties, training presentation, discipline/refusal rules, battle formula and random final-evolution gates. Original sound, connector interoperability and physical hardware behavior are not reproduced. Signed iOS, physical iPhone and Home Screen widget checks remain separate from JavaScript exports.
