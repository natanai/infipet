Care Mistakes

How many care mistakes are required to evolve. The number of full skulls indicates how many care mistakes are required, while half skulls indicate that a care mistake is optional. In the example to the left, 1 care mistake is required, but 2 or 3 will also work, though 4 will not. If 4 Care Mistakes are indicated as working for evolution, more than 4 Care Mistakes will also work.

Training

How many training sessions are required to evolve. In the example to the left, you must train between 5 and 15 times to fullill this condition.

Overfeed

Your Digimon only has 4 Hunger hearts, but it can eat more more than four times. If your Digimon does not have 4 Hunger hearts, keep feeding it meat until it refuses to eat anymore. That counts as one Overfeed. In order to Overfeed again, you must wait until it loses at least one Hunger heart. Keep in mind that Overfeeding only matters if your Child Digimon has 4 or more Care Mistakes. If it has 3 or fewer Care Mistakes, Overfeeding is not considered. It does not matter whether you Overfeed before or after getting 4 Care Mistakes.

Sleep Disturbance

How many times you have awoken your Digimon after it falls asleep. You can wake a sleeping Digimon by attempting to feed, train or battle with it. Keep in mind that Sleep Disturbances only matter if your Child Digimon has 4 or more Care Mistakes. If it has 3 or fewer Care Mistakes, Sleep Disturbances are not considered. It does not matter whether you disturb sleep before or after getting 4 Care Mistakes

Other Conditions Not Met

If you raise a Digimon that doesn't meet the requirements for other evolutions, it will follow this path. For example, if you have Betamon, and you get 4 care mistakes, but you train only 7 times, you will get Numemon. You don't get Devimon or Meramon since they require 2 or fewer Care Mistakes, and you don't get Airdramon or Seadramon since they require 8-31 training.

Fulfill Battles

The Digital Monster follows a very specific battle logic, and the maximum chance of evolution into Perfect is 75%. In order to reach that 75% chance, you must first do 15 battles during Stage III. Second, you have to make sure your Win Score is 9 or higher when your Digimon is evolving from Stage III to Stage IV AND from Stage IV to Stage V. Win Score is increased by 1 every time you Win a battle, and decreased by 1 every time you Lose a battle. Win Score does not reset when evolving, so if you do 15 battles as a Child and have a Win Score of 9 or higher when you evolve to Adult, you do not need to do any more battles to meet the requirement. See the FAQ for more details on lower percentages.
If you aren't sure about something, take a minute and read through the questions below, you may find your answer there. If not, try asking in the #help_and_questions channel at the Digitama Hatchery Discord Server.
  • What counts as a care mistake?
    • A care mistake occurs any time the Call Light goes out without the Digimon's need being taken care of. Your Digimon will call when its Hunger or Strength Hearts empty, or when it is time for it to go to bed. You have 20 minutes to refill hearts or put a Digimon to bed before the Call Light goes out and you get a Care Mistake. If Hunger and Strength empty at the same time, this may only count as one Care Mistake, but more testing is needed to be sure. Waking a sleeping Digimon does not count as a Care Mistake, but instead counts as a Sleep Disturbance. Poop and Injuries are not Care Mistakes.
  • How long will it take my Digimon to evolve?
    • Evolution times will vary depending on the Digimon, and specific timings may be found in the Evolution Guide. Rough timeframes for each evolution stage are as follows:
    Current Stage Time to Next Stage
    I 1 Hour
    II 20 - 44 Hours
    III 68 - 76 Hours
    IV 44 - 124 Hours
    V 48 Hours
    Unless you get 20 Care Mistakes or Injuries, your Digimon is guaranteed to evolve into Stage IV. If a Stage IV Digimon has fulfilled its specific requirements for reaching Stage V, it has at most a 75% chance of evolution.
  • What carries over after evolution?
    • The number of battles and your Win Score will carry over. Care Mistakes and Injuries both reset upon evolution.
  • What does it mean if the evolution requirements on this guide show multiple bullet points?
    • When you click on a Digimon in the guide, sometimes you will see bullet points listing multiple requirements. In these cases, you only need to choose one of the bullet points, as these are all independent requirements. For example, Betamon has several bullet points for evolving into Numemon. One reads "4+ Care Mistakes, 0-7 Training" while another reads "4+ Care Mistakes, 32+ Training". You will meet the requirement by fulfilling either of those conditions when the evolution timer runs out (In this case it would also be impossible to fulfill both bullet points at once, since you cannot have 0-7 training AND more than 32 training at the same time).
  • If I did 15 battles during Stage III, do I have to do 15 more during Stage IV to evolve into Stage V?
    • No, doing 15 battles as during Stage III meets the number of battles requirement, but keep your Win Score in mind.
  • OK so what exactly is Win Score, and how is the chance to evolve into Stage V calculated?
    • It's a little complicated! There are two main stats to keep track of:

    • Number of Battles: Exactly what it says, this stat is how many times you have battled, regardless of whether you win or lose. This number never decreases and is not reset when evolving.
    • Win Score: Your Win Score starts at 0 and goes up by 1 every time you Win a battle. Every time you Lose a battle, it goes down by 1. It can't go below 0 and it can't go above 15.

    Neither of those stats is visible on the device, so you will need to track them yourself. If you forget your current Win Score, you can always win more battles to be safe, just remember that your losses decrease it. So how do those stats get used? When it is time for your Stage III Digimon to evolve, it does a check to see if you have done 15 battles. If you did, it then checks your current Win Score and uses that to set your odds for evolving to Stage V. A Win Score of 0-4 gives a 1/8 chance of evolutiopn, 5-8 gives 1/4, and 9-15 gives 1/2. A roll is performed and if its successul then your Digimon will evolve into Stage V when the time comes. This process happens again when the time comes for your Stage IV Digimon to evolve. The number of battles for the check includes battles done during Stage III, so you do not need to do 15 more during stage IV. If you did do battles during Stage IV, your wins and losses will of course affect your Win Score for the next check. This means you get two times where the device determines whether you will evolve into Stage V, and you will only need one of those two checks to be successful, nothing else matters. The highest chance of success is 50% per check. Since the check is performed twice, that means your overall chance of success maxxes out at 75%. If you fail both checks, you Digimon will not evolve to Stage V.

    Below is a table showing the chance of success, assuming that you have done 15 battles with your Digimon. The left column is your Win Score when evolving from Stage III to Stage IV, and the top row is your Win Score when evolving from Stage IV to V. The percentage where your two Win Scores intersect is your combined chance of evolution.
    III \ IV 0-4 5-8 9-15
    0-4 23.44% 34.38% 56.25%
    5-8 34.38% 43.75% 62.50%
    9-15 56.25% 62.50% 75.00%
  • Do failed trainings count toward evolution?
    • Failed or canceled training will only count for evolution on the Ver.1, all other versions require the training to be successful in order for it to count for evolution.
  • How do I get a traited egg?
    • As long as your Digimon exceeds its natural lifespan before it dies, it will leave behind a traited egg. Whether this egg does anything is unclear, but it does not appear to affect evolution or battle logic.
  • What will cause my Digimon to die?
    • Getting sick ?? times in one form (Not sure of the exact number, may be as low as 10)
    • Remaining injured for 6 consecutive hours in one form
    • Leaving the Hunger or Strength hearts empty for 6 consecutive hours in one form
    • Having 5 or more Care Mistakes when or after your Digimon's lifespan is up

  • Can the Digital Monster Color connect to older two-pronged V-Pets?
    • No, the Digital Monster Color can only battle against other devices in the Digital Monster Color series and the Digimon Pendulum Color series.
  • Does Attribute matter?
    • Nope! Attribute wasn't introduced to the Digimon Franchise until the Digimon Pendulum series, so technically none of the Digimon on the Digital Monster have an attribute. However, the detail cards will still include the attribute used for the Digital Monster Ver.20th, Digimon Pendulum or Reference Book. The inclusion of attributes on this guide is purely for cosmetic purposes.
*Win Score starts and 0, increases by 1 for each Win and decreases by 1 for each Loss. It cannot go below 0 or above 15.
